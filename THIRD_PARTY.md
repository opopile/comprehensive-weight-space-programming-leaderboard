# Third-Party Notices & Attributions

This repository consolidates public Weight-Space Programming (WSP) MinAtar Experiment 5 benchmark results alongside original Gemini 4 Neon (XHigh) experimental artifacts, verification scripts, and bilingual documentation. All external benchmarks, papers, datasets, community replications, and runtime dependencies retain their respective upstream licenses and copyrights.

- Users who run `scripts/verify_artifacts.py` with live simulation enabled install `MinAtar==1.0.15` directly from PyPI under the terms of `GPL-3.0`.
